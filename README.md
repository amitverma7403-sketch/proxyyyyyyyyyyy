# NodeMax

Share your phone's unused internet and get paid for every GB it carries.

## Download

Latest APK: https://github.com/amitverma7403-sketch/proxyyyyyyyyyyy/releases/latest

Install `NodeMax.apk`, create an account with your mobile number, and tap Connect.

## What the app does

While you are connected, other NodeMax customers' internet traffic is sent through your phone's connection and leaves
from your IP address. This uses your mobile data or Wi-Fi. You choose when to connect and can disconnect at any time.
Banking, payment and adult sites are blocked on the network.

## Permissions

Internet, network state, notifications, a foreground service while connected, wake lock, start after reboot, and
basic phone state (to tell 4G from 5G). No camera, location, contacts, storage or root access.

## Security

- HTTPS only; user-installed certificates are not trusted by the app.
- Release builds are signed with one fixed key. Check with `apksigner verify --print-certs NodeMax.apk`.
- No backups of app data, and no exported components apart from the launcher.
- Report problems privately to the repository owner.
